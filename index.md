# Wide Open Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

This policy covers the Android app published as **Wide Open** (package `com.treyfishing.app`).

## Summary
The app has no accounts and no ads. It does not collect, share or sell personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## Data you enter
Trips, packing lists, tackle, and catch logs (including photos you choose) are stored only on your device. We have no servers and cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. You can export your own data as CSV from Settings; the export does not include catch pins. If you use Android's backup, Google may back up app data, including saved catch pins, under your own Google account settings.

## Photos
When you attach a photo to a catch, the app opens Android's system photo picker. It receives only the photos you select. It requests no broad photo or camera permission. If you tap "Use photo's location", the app asks for Android's photo location permission (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) so it can read the place the photo was taken from the photo itself. That position stays on your device and is saved only if you save it as the catch pin.

## Sharing
The catch share card is created only when you tap Share. It goes to the app you choose in Android's share sheet and does not include catch pins. The app does not share anything on its own. Two other actions hand information to another app only when you start them: Add to calendar sends the trip's title, place and notes to your calendar app, and opening a catch pin opens it in your maps app.

## Network requests
The app has the INTERNET permission to download public conditions data (such as NOAA buoy, tide, satellite ocean temperature, chlorophyll and surface current data, National Weather Service forecasts, and Open-Meteo weather). These requests use the preset coordinates (or preset station and office IDs) of the fishing area you follow, not your phone's location, and they do not identify you. As with any web request, the servers that answer can see your device's IP address, and the app does not send them anything else about you. No account, advertising ID or personal details are sent. Links to outside websites (landings and booking pages, official fishing regulations and species sources) open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies; map links open your maps app.

## Location
Location is optional. The app may offer location access when you first open it, and you can decline; you can change this later in Settings or by tapping a location button. Your phone's location is used on your device only. It is read when you tap "Use my location": to set a catch pin, to find the nearest fishing area for Conditions, to choose the area for a trip, and to find your fishing regulations area. It is also read when you start a new trip if you have already allowed location access, to preselect the nearest fishing area. A position is saved only when you save a catch pin, and it is never sent to us or anyone else. Picking the nearest area uses the position once and does not save it.

## Feedback
Settings has a Send feedback screen. A report you write is saved on your phone and, when you choose to send it, it opens your own email app with the text filled in. The app itself sends nothing; you decide whether to send the email. Giving contact details is optional, and you can choose whether to include a line with the app version, Android version and phone model.

## Notifications
The trip notification is optional. The app asks for notification permission only when you tap "Show on lock screen" on the On the water card. It shows your current trip on the lock screen; if your lock screen hides sensitive content, it shows only "On the water" and the back time. It goes away when the trip ends, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Ads and analytics
The app contains no advertising. It keeps simple counts of which features you use on your device to help improve the app; these counts are not sent anywhere.

## Children
The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
